Renders NOTHING when priority is unset -- that is a real state (nobody has ranked this), and printing a number for it would be the board inventing a decision no one made. Deliberately no tier name. The NUMBERS are the vocabulary and Org.priority_policy supplies their meaning, so shipping "Urgent"/"High" here would put a second vocabulary on the same screen as whatever the person wrote in their own policy -- and a classifying agent would have two to obey. `policy_line` is the one line of the policy that explains this number.
